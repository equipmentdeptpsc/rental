Status: decision package only

Approval fields are intentionally blank.

### Summary

The Administrator mapping is technically acceptable subject to continuing separation-of-duty controls. Finance, Management, and Rental Operations remain blocked pending explicit business decisions. Recommendations below are least-privilege proposals, not approvals and not authority changes.

### Administrator Resolution

Proposed mapping: `system-administrator` → `system-administrator`.

- Legacy authority: all 38 legacy catalog permissions, including broad user, role, settings, master-data, operational, and financial administration.
- Canonical authority: all 157 active canonical permissions.
- Equivalence rationale: both roles are deliberately unrestricted application administrators. Canonical permission decomposition expands vocabulary without reducing administrative coverage; compatibility aliases explain the retired broad codes.
- Acceptability: acceptable only for the minimum number of designated administrators. It does not justify using the role for ordinary operational work.
- Required exceptions and controls: same-tenant enforcement; no secret, token, credential, provider-body, or encryption-material read access; prohibition on approving one's own privilege escalation; final-active-administrator protection; and independent approval/audit for high-risk financial, backup/restore, scheduler, and authorization-catalog changes.

Recommendation: **APPROVE**, subject to the controls above and named-owner sign-off.

Least-privilege disposition: retain Billing Staff only after removing approval, terminal, destructive, reversal, and general export authority from its base role or moving those permissions to a separately assigned financial-approver/export role. - Operations Manager loses `equipment.delete` and `dailyLog.manage`.
- Dispatcher loses `equipment.create`, `equipment.update`, `equipment.delete`, `equipment.restore`, `rental.release`, `rental.return`, `deur.create`, `deur.review`, `deur.correct`, `maintenance.manage`, `dailyLog.manage`, `billing.read`, and `collections.read`.
- Equipment Coordinator loses Rental management/release/return/approval/commercial terms, DEUR create/review/correct, customer/project/operator management, daily-log management, billing read, and collections read.
- Operator loses 18 broad operational capabilities and additionally requires an active canonical Operator link.
- Maintenance Staff loses 19 dispatch, Rental, DEUR, customer/project, billing, and collection capabilities.

Recommended shortlist:

1. **Dispatcher** if the user primarily plans assignments and Rentals. This has the smallest non-Operator privilege increase, but requires explicit decisions for every lost duty.
2. **Dispatcher + Equipment Coordinator** only if both dispatch and fleet-custody duties are documented.
3. **Operations Manager** only if the user is the designated approver/closer and the 37 additions are individually approved or removed through role redesign.

Operator is not currently eligible because there is no Operator linkage. Maintenance Staff should be added only with evidence of maintenance duties. No combination is approved automatically.
